# Funnier

Arena for comedy. Two YouTube clips, one vote: which is funnier?

- Live: https://www.funnier.si/ (Cloudflare Worker `funnier-web` proxying https://prx0r.github.io/characters/; the apex 301s to www). API: https://api.funnier.si
- Votes are AT Protocol records (`io.github.prx0r.funnier.vote`) written to each voter's own Bluesky repo via OAuth.
  Record: `{ a, b, winner, createdAt }` (+ optional `voter`, `basis`, `why`, `ms`, `daily` (YYYY-MM-DD daily clash), `cc` (opt-in ISO country, daily picks only)) where a/b/winner are YouTube video ids from `clips.json`.
- `.github/workflows/funnier.yml` runs every 15 min: discovers voters on Jetstream, recounts every voter's records
  from their PDS (`tally/tally.mjs`), computes Elo (K=32) + Wilson lower bound for clips and comics (cross-comic votes only),
  and publishes `data/leaderboard.json` + `data/votes.json` to the `gh-pages` branch. One vote per voter per pair (latest wins).
- Clips: any embeddable YouTube upload of a stand-up set (decided 2026-10-16), official or not, embedded with YouTube's
  own player; nothing downloaded. Each clip has `carrier`: `own` (the comic's channel or "<Comic> - Topic"), `licensed`
  (Netflix / Netflix Is A Joke, Comedy Central, JFL, HBO, Team Coco, late-night shows, SNL, Kill Tony; list in
  `channels.json` `licensedCarriers`) or `unofficial` (everything else). Add clips to `clips.json`.
- Unofficial uploads are embedded via YouTube's player; rights holders can request removal; dead embeds are pruned daily.
- Build frontend: `npm i && npm run build` (bundles `src/app.js` -> `site/app.js`).

## Algorithms (v2)
- **Ranking:** hierarchical Bradley-Terry fitted by MAP over all votes, so vote order doesn't matter. Clip strength = comic strength + clip offset, with the offset shrunk toward 0 (λ=0.5).
  95% intervals from 200 bootstrap resamples. Elo-scale output (400/ln10), mean comic = 1500. Three boards: all, human, agent.
- **Pair selection (`data/pairs.json`):** weight = 0.5·closeness·uncertainty + 0.5·freshness, × 1/(1+times paired), × 1.25 when the comics differ.
  Pairs stay in the same lane (human or ai) and the same length band (<2 min, 2–8, 8+) when lengths are known. The client samples the top 40 by weight and explores at random 20% of the time.
- **Screening flow ("Tonight's Screening"):** face-off intro (~2.5s, tap to skip) → clip ONE full-screen (until YouTube ENDED or the 75s cap) → clip TWO the same way → 3-second ballot with a FUNNIER button under each thumbnail. No tap in 3s = a local pass (`funnier.passes`), no effect on either comic. Swipe up / wheel / ↑ / PageDown / "Next clash ↑" leaves at any stage and slides in the next face-off.
- **Fullscreen logo:** in fullscreen, while the chrome is hidden, the FUNNIER logo stays pinned top-left at 0.3 opacity (slightly desaturated) and returns to full on mouse move or hover.
- **Chrome (premium declutter):** the top bar is only the FUNNIER logo, the tabs and a round account icon (silhouette when signed out). Lineup (human / AI / human vs AI), History and an "Advanced" Bluesky publishing option live in the account menu. No volume slider and no share button before a vote; sharing appears in the after-vote card and in History. The intro runs on its own and clip ONE starts automatically: if the browser refuses audible autoplay the clip plays muted with one centred "Tap for sound" pill, and after any tap later clips play with sound.
- **Intro face-off:** each card shows the clip's own YouTube thumbnail (hq720, falling back to hqdefault), with the comic's name, tagline and crowd rating in a strip *below* the picture. "Tonight's screening" is a small kicker and the round mode ("Exploring something new", "Picked for you"…) a small pill on a bar above the cards, never over them; the marquee no longer repeats the mode. Phones (≤640px) stack the cards with VS between them.
- **Not funny → next (clip skip):** during clip ONE a "Not funny → #2" button (bottom-left of the theatre, and in the auto-hiding menu), → / N, or a left swipe ends clip one and starts clip two. During clip TWO the same control ("Not funny → vote") goes straight to the ballot. Logged in `funnier.skips` as stage `left-skip` / `right-skip` with `clip`, `comic`, `style`, `watchedSec`, and counted as a weak loss (0.4 of a pick) for that clip vs an average clip, its comic and its style tags (mid-clip "Next clash" skips stay at 0.3). The ballot still follows. A skipped clip doesn't need its 30 s, but the other clip still does, and skipping both makes the pick a local partial. A counted vote carries `leftSkipped` / `rightSkipped`; after a left-skip, picking clip TWO weighs max(timing, 1.2) and picking clip ONE anyway weighs min(timing, 0.6) (mirrored after a right-skip).
- **Volume (honest version):** we can't read audio from the cross-origin YouTube player, and downloading or analysing a clip's audio is off-limits. YouTube already loudness-normalises uploads, but only by turning loud ones *down*; the IFrame API's `setVolume(0–100)` can likewise only attenuate. So a clip's volume = clamp(`vol` (clips.json, default 100) × (100 + crowd offset)/100 + your local offset, 0, 100). **The real fix is the calibration pass:** `site/calibrate.html` (unlisted; open once with `?key=<anything>` to unlock that browser) alternates each clip's screening window with a reference clip in the official player, gives a 0–100 slider per clip, and prints a JSON patch of `vol` values to paste into clips.json and commit. **Crowd offset (automatic, silent):** the ballot's "#1/#2 too loud / too quiet" taps keep a local offset (`funnier.audio`, ±15) and also go to `POST /clips/audio {clip, dir, device}` (one vote per person per clip, latest wins). `GET /clips/audio` estimates each clip's level in 3 dB steps (`steps = round(clamp((loud − quiet)/√n, −4, 4))`, from 3 votes), normalises to the quietest clip (`ref_dB` = the lowest level), and returns `offset = 100·10^((ref_dB − clip_dB)/20) − 100`, clamped −40…0, plus `default_offset` for clips nobody rated. "Too quiet" can't push a clip above 100, so it lowers everything else instead.
- **Clip length fairness:** a 20-minute clip never meets a 4-minute one on unequal terms. Each clip has a *screening window* = editorial `start`…`end` when set, else `min(dur, SHORT_MAX = 75s)` from `start` (default 0). Two clips are paired only when their windows are within 1.5× of each other, and both then play the **same** length: the shorter window (at least 30s, never past a clip's own window). `dur` (seconds) is in clips.json where known; otherwise each viewer's player reports `getDuration()` once (cached in `funnier.durs`, `POST /clips/durations`), and `GET /clips/durations` fills unknown lengths at load (clips.json always wins). Clips over 3 min without an editorial `start` are `needsCut: true` (set in clips.json, or derived at runtime from a learned length) and are only paired when no fair alternative is left. Shared, daily and rewatch clashes skip the pairing filter but still play equal windows. To fix a needsCut clip, add `start` (and ideally `end`) at its best bit.
- **Progress bar:** shows progress through the screening window (start to end), driven by `getCurrentTime()` at 4 Hz with a 250 ms linear CSS transition. It's a slider with a 24px hit area over a 3px line: tap to seek, drag to scrub (mouse or touch, pointer events with capture), ←/→ on the focused bar = ±5s; `,`/`.` or `J`/`L` = −10s/+10s anywhere in the theatre. Seeking forward sets `skippedAhead`. Touches on the bar never start a swipe.
- **Laugh button (v0):** one round red 😂-style button bottom-centre in the theatre. Each tap stores `{clip, t, view}` in `funnier.laughs` and sends `POST /laughs {clip, t, device}` (anonymous allowed with a random device id; signed-in laughs count per user; one per 0.7s, enforced on both sides). Your tap pops a small burst. `GET /laughs/:clip` returns a 1-second histogram; while you watch, other viewers' laughs (minus your own) float up as small particles at those seconds, density scaled to the clip's busiest second. "Crowd laughs" toggle (top bar, auto-hides with the chrome; `funnier.crowdLaughs`, default on). Visual only, never a laugh track. Model: each laugh = +0.15 of a vote for that clip, its comic and its styles (a weak win vs an average clip), capped at 1.0 per clip per viewing. "Your laughs" shows on Your model. The button never triggers a swipe.
- **Watch gate:** a pick only counts if both clips played for min(30s, 80% of the screened length) or to the end, tracked with the YouTube IFrame API; otherwise it is kept locally in `funnier.partials` and not published.
- **Personal taste (`data/taste/<did>.json`):** per-voter comic ratings, empirical-Bayes shrunk toward the crowd (prior strength 2). `vsCrowd` shows where a voter's taste differs from everyone else's.
- **Voters:** humans via the web OAuth client. Agents write records directly with `voter: agent` and a model (see AGENTS-VOTING.md and `lexicons/`). Agent status sticks to the account.
- **Lanes:** each clips.json entry has `kind` (human or ai) and `media` (youtube, or audio with `src`). The arena has Human sets / AI sets / Human vs AI lanes.
- **Catalog (`tally/ingest.mjs`, daily, no API key):** re-checks every clip with oEmbed and marks dead or non-embeddable ones `status: removed`. It also reads the RSS feeds of the channels allowlisted in `channels.json` and adds matching uploads to `candidates.json` for human review.
- **Implicit signals (local, no laugh button):** each pick stores `decisionMs` (from the moment the Funnier buttons appear to the click, not from page load), `leftWatchedSec` / `rightWatchedSec` (seconds actually played, accumulated from the IFrame API's `getCurrentTime` every 250 ms), `rightFrac` (share of the screened part of clip two that was watched), `replayed` (either clip seeked back >2 s) and `skippedAhead` (jumped forward >5 s). "Skip pair" is logged separately (`funnier.skips`) with the same signals.
  Confidence weight per pick: ×1.25 if decided in <1.5 s (snap), ×0.7 if >12 s, else ×1.0; picking clip ONE in <3 s after watching <60% of clip TWO counts as strong (×1.4). Picks without signals weigh 1. Skips (`funnier.skips`: pairing, stage intro/left/right/vote, comics on screen, seconds watched) made during a clip count as a weak loss (0.3 of a pick) for that clip vs an average clip, which also lowers its comic and its style tags; intro/ballot skips are stored but never penalise. The weight multiplies each pick's gradient in the personal Bradley-Terry fit and the style-trait regression. Only `ms` is published (optional integer on the vote record); the tally keeps it sanitised in `votes.json` but the public charts ignore it for now.
- **Specials log:** `comics.json` lists each comic's verified specials (`specials: [{ id, title, year, distributor, runtimeMin?, where: [{ name, url }] }]`); `where` holds only the official home (Netflix originals, louisck.com) plus a JustWatch search because availability varies by country. Seen / 0.5–5 stars / note are stored on-device (`funnier.specials`).
- **Test:** `SYNTH=300 node tally/tally.mjs clips.json /tmp/out` runs the tally on synthetic votes.

## Daily clash
`site/daily.json` is the editorial schedule: `[{ date, title, blurb, a, b, topic }]`. Titles are short and punchy ("Holy Roasts", "Lockdown Lunacy"); the screening shows one compact banner, "Daily Clash: <title>" with a countdown (fallback days read just "Daily Clash"). Days without an entry fall back to a topic-matched pair chosen by an FNV-1a hash of the date (identical logic in `src/app.js` and `tally/tally.mjs`). The tally writes `data/daily.json` with per-day totals, % split, and per-country splits (a country appears once it has 5+ votes; the rest is "Other").

## Screening UX (v4.1)
- Sound: every clip tries to autoplay WITH sound; if the browser refuses (not playing ~500ms in, after a few looks for a still-loading player) it plays muted, silently. There is no sound prompt. The first interaction anywhere (pointer, touch, key, wheel, scroll, a vote, a swipe) unmutes the playing clip with the fade, primes the clip waiting next in the clash (silent play + pause, for iOS) and stores `funnier.sound = "on"`; later clips start unmuted. A small speaker in the theatre chrome (or M) toggles mute; muting stores `funnier.sound = "off"`, respected on every later clip and visit until unmuted. Players are still one iframe per clip per clash (reusing one player across clashes via `loadVideoById` would need a persistent theatre layer; not done).
- Ballot: 7s (`VOTE_MS`) with a thin draining ring; it holds while the mouse is over the ballot or a finger is on it (and after a sound-feedback tap), resuming with at least 2.5s. No pick = pass.
- Pairing hard rules, at every fallback level (client `openPair`, tally `pairs.json`, the daily fallback in both): two different comics, plus the duration-fairness rule; struct_sim only ranks cross-comic pairs. Shared/rewatched clashes are exempt (the user chose them). Tests: smoke `basic` samples 500 rounds; `node tally/test-pairs.mjs` samples 500 weighted rounds from a synthetic tally.
- Picks vote on `pointerup` (click is the fallback); swipes never start on a button. Browser check: `api/test/ballot-browser.mjs` (headless Chromium, stub YouTube API: real finger taps with 22px drift, mouse clicks, autoplay with sound, refused-sound silent fallback + unmute on the first wheel, 9:16 player).
- Device-aware: `FORMAT_PREFS` in `src/app.js`: mobile (coarse pointer + portrait, or < 700px wide) prefers `format: "vertical"` (Shorts) for 80% of rounds and screens up to 75s; desktop prefers horizontal (90%) and screens up to 150s; a format with fewer than 6 clips in the lane falls back to all clips. The duration-fairness rule applies on top. `node tools/detect-format.mjs` sets `format` keylessly (oEmbed taller than wide, or `/shorts/<id>` answering 200 instead of a 303 to /watch). The editorial field formerly called `format` (talk-show, stand-up, chat) is now `setting`. Vertical clips render in a 9:16 player at full height.

## Theory base from characters2 (v4)
- `site/theory.json` is generated by `node tools/import-theory.mjs [--c2 <characters2 clone>]` (default `../../characters2`, or `$CHARACTERS2`) from prx0r/characters2: `theory/comedians/<slug>/comedian-graph.json` (delivery archetype, numeric MARY coords only where measured, energy, punchline hold; 7 comics have graphs), and `theory/transcripts/ml/` `operator_frequency.json` (measured operator priors, top 5), `stylometry.json`, `laugh_density.json` (summary), plus each matched special's operator mix counted from `bits_annotated`. It records the characters2 commit it came from. No transcript or bit text is shipped; the bits corpus stays in the characters2 clone and is read offline only. Re-run the importer after characters2 changes or after adding clips/comics, then `node tools/test-theory.mjs`.
- Clip -> special join (§2 method 1, metadata only): the importer sets `special_ref` (our `comics.json` special id) and `special_id` (the characters2 transcript slug, or `null`) when the clip TITLE names a special (outtakes never count; a bare year is too generic). When the title's own topic words cluster in one place of that special's bits, it also sets an approximate `t_start` (seconds into the special, `t_source: "title-anchor"`). Nothing is fetched from YouTube; no captions or audio are used.
- struct_sim (§4a, `src/structsim.js`, shared by the client and the tally): weighted blend of style Jaccard 0.25, operator overlap 0.25 (cosine of each operator mix's lift over the 18-comic mean, since every comic leans on split_knowledge), topic Jaccard 0.15, stylometry closeness 0.15 (z-scored), archetype match 0.15, MARY closeness 0.05; components a pair lacks drop out and the weights renormalise; special-level ops/stylometry when both clips have a special. For pairing, same-comic pairs drop comic-level traits (identical by construction). Tally `pairs.json`: `w = (0.4·close·unc + 0.3·fresh + 0.3·struct_sim)·unseen·cross`, each pair also carries `s` (its struct_sim); the length band stays a hard rule. Client: For you picks the opponent by struct_sim minus model familiarity; Exploring pairs the least-seen clip with a kindred clip from another comic; the fair-duration rule in `openPair` is unchanged.
- contentSim (§4b, `src/app.js`): For you scores each clip as `MODEL.score + traitScore + contentSim + 0.3·random`, where contentSim is the pick-confidence-weighted mean struct_sim (recommendation mode, so same-comic kinship counts) against your up-to-8 most-liked clips, excluding the clip itself.
- Time map (§3c): `tally/bitmap.mjs` maps video time t in a clip to the bits on screen: special time = `t_start` + t, pos = special time / `specials[].durSec` (runtime from comics.json, else words/150wpm), bits whose `pos_in_special` falls in the window.

## Profiles: show off your sense of humour (v5, "Letterboxd for comedy")
- **Public profile** at `www.funnier.si/u/<username>` (in-app `#u/<username>`; your own is `#profile`, "My profile" in the account menu). `funnier-web` serves `/u/<name>` as the app shell with `<base href="/">` plus per-profile `og:`/`twitter:` tags, and `/u/<name>/og.png` (the owner's card from the API, else `site/img/og-default.png`); the app turns the path into `#u/<name>`. `site/404.html` does the same redirect on prx0r.github.io. Sections: mascot + username, **Four Favourites** (comics.json; first thing in the onboarding sheet, editable on the profile), **Sense of humour** (the chart), **Pinned clips** (≤6), **Specials diary** (seen / ★ / note, newest first), **My sets** (empty state "AI sets coming soon"; table `sets`, kind `ai`, nothing writes it yet), and for the owner: friends/taste match, invites, privacy.
- **Anonymous users** get the same profile from this device (`funnier.favs`, `funnier.pins`, `funnier.specials`); signing in publishes it (newest edit wins, both ways; the diary merges per special by `at`).
- **The humour chart** (`src/humour.js`, pure, shared SVG component `chartSVG(series)`): 8 axes (Absurd / anti-joke, Deadpan, Observational, Dark, Storytelling, Wordplay & one-liners, Rant, Self-roast; Crowd work appears once clips carry a `crowd-work` style tag). Each clip's features = its style tags + 0.35 × a theory prior (characters2 delivery archetype and operator lift over the roster mean, special-level when matched). A dimension's value is a shrunk win rate: Σ pick-confidence × feature of the winner / Σ over both sides (+1/+2), skips count as losses (0.3/0.4), each Four Favourite as 0.6 of a pick. 50 = neutral. Also: favourite moves (operator win rates; `GET /me/humour` weights blend in when the offline fit exists, its line shows as "From your rewinds"), stylometry leans (energy, punchline hold, pace) from theory.json, "<pct>% <comic>-adjacent" (cosine of your leans vs each comic's centred profile), "Funniest to you" (your model's top seen clip), and a "Make me laugh by…" line. Works with zero server data. Test: `node tools/test-humour-chart.mjs`.
- **Friends overlay** `#vs/<name>[,<name>…]` (≤3): your chart over theirs, taste match = mean of chart similarity (`tasteMatch`) and agreement on shared clashes (`/me/taste`, from 3 shared), "You both love…" / "You disagree on…" lines. Friend chips come from the invite friendships.
- **Share cards** (`src/cards.js`, canvas → PNG; Web Share with the file on phones, download on desktop): humour chart (1080×1350 feed and 1080×1920 story), "Make me laugh by…" (dating-prompt style), "Funny / Not funny" (Shuffle), "My comedy taste in 4 comics", "Me vs my friend" (from the overlay). Only our mascots, type and same-origin Commons portraits (credited on the card); no meme templates, video frames or YouTube thumbnails. Every card has a `funnier.si/u/<username>` watermark. Entry points: profile hero, the chart, the overlay, and a small "Share your taste ↗" under the after-vote card (from 5 picks).
- **Link previews:** the owner's browser draws a 1200×630 card from their chart and uploads it (`POST /me/og`, PNG ≤1 MB, only when the chart changed and the profile is public); `GET /og/u/<name>.png` serves it.
- **API:** `GET|PUT /me/profile-data` (favs ≤4, pins ≤6, `chart` snapshot sanitised by `cleanChart`, `vis_profile` / `vis_diary` = public | friends | private; defaults public profile + chart, friends-only diary), `PUT /me/specials` (batch, newest `at` wins), `POST /me/og`, `GET /u/:username` (case-insensitive; strangers see what's public, friends also friends-only, owner all; never email or stats), `GET /og/u/:name.png`. Migration `0006_profiles.sql` (tables `profiles`, `specials_log`, `sets`; idempotent). `DELETE /me` removes them too. Browser test: `node test/profile-browser.mjs` (renders every card to PNG in Chromium, checks the `/u/<name>` shell and the OG upload).
